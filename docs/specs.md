# Talent templates (specs)

the owner, 2026-11-12 (DQ4): the guild leader assigns the specs; specs can be taken from the wowbot repository; every class
has a default when nothing is chosen; templates per class (levelling, dungeon, raid ...) can be created and assigned to
bots. `SpecService.h/.cpp`, built 2026-11-12 on branch `claude/project-thread-xovemk`.

## Why

mod-playerbots chose the talents of the guild bots with its premade specs (`AutoPickTalentsForPlayerControlledBots`),
by its default tree per class: holy for priests. Over 1050 fights against one foe on the dev instance the holy priests
spent 66 % of their mana per foe of their level and lost 13 of 114 such fights; the rogue lost 44 % of its health per
foe. mod-playerbots also chooses its combat strategies (damage, tank, healer: its rotation) from the tree with the most
talent points, so the talents decide how a bot fights.

## Stored strategies

mod-playerbots chooses a bot's combat strategies (its rotation) from the tree with the most talent points when the
bot logs in, and then loads what it stored for that bot at an earlier logout (`playerbots_db_store`, keys `co` / `nc`)
over them. The module stores at every clean logout too. So a bot kept the combat strategies of the day they were
first stored: on the integration server of 2026-11-12 a priest with Shadow talents fought with `holy heal, healer dps,
save mana`, and a Feral druid with `balance` (stored before its first talent point; it cast Wrath in every fight). A
server that stops without logging the bots out never corrected it.

`BotDriver::SyncStrategies` ends that: when an AI-played guild character enters the world, and after a level-up
once its fight is over, the strategies are taken from the talents again and stored, and the activity log says what
changed ("plays by its talents again: combat strategies healer dps, holy heal, save mana -> shadow, shadow aoe,
shadow debuff"). Assigning a template stores the new strategies at once. A strategy the owner switched on by a chat
command to the bot is lost at its next login; the template is how a guild's bots are told what to play.

## Templates

A template is a list of talent points in the order they are spent, level 10 first, each point as `tab.row.column`
(all from 0).

- **Built-in** (world table `ga_spec_template`, 46 rows): generated by `tools/gen_spec_templates.py` from the talent
  profiles of the wowbot335 repository (`profiles/talents/*.json`, one point per level from 10 to 80). `solo` profiles
  are "Leveling: ...", `group` profiles "Dungeon and raid: ...", `pvp` "PvP: ...". Columns: `class`, `code` (the
  profile's file name), `name`, `purpose` (0 leveling, 1 dungeon and raid, 2 raid, 3 PvP), `role` (0 damage, 1 tank,
  2 healer), `tab` (the tree with the most points at level 60), `is_default`, `points`. The ids are fixed in the
  generator (class × 100 + n); regenerate the file after a change of the profiles, never renumber.
  One change against the profiles, for this server's level cap of 60 (51 points; since 2026-11-25, after the owner found
  Blood death knights without Heart Strike and Restoration druids without Tree of Life): a profile is made for level 80
  and takes some of the talents a spec is played with only after point 51. The generator reads `Talent.dbc` and
  `Spell.dbc` (the server's `Data\dbc`) and moves every **key talent** to the earliest point its tier, its
  prerequisites and its rank allow: every active talent the profile takes (a single-rank talent that gives a spell to
  cast; not Focus Magic and Typhoon, `KEY_SKIP`), the last talent (row 10) of a tree that has the first 50 points
  (Beast Mastery for the hunter's exotic pet, Dispersion, Metamorphosis ...), and the talents of `KEY_ADDITIONS`, which
  the profile does not take although mod-playerbots' rotation of the spec casts them (Hysteria for Blood, Frost Strike
  for Frost, Bone Shield for Unholy, Survival Instincts for the three Feral templates, Thunderstorm for Elemental,
  Cleanse Spirit for Restoration shamans, Shadowfury for Destruction). A key talent that comes into the level-60 part
  from later pushes the last other point of that part that nothing depends on out to point 52, so every template
  still has 51 points at 60 and its order can be learned point by point (the generator checks both). At 60 this gives
  Heart Strike, Vampiric Blood and Hysteria to Blood, Frost Strike, Unbreakable Armor and Hungering Cold to Frost, Bone
  Shield to Unholy, Tree of Life to Restoration druids, Circle of Healing to Holy priests, Divine Sacrifice to
  Protection paladins and the additions above; what goes out is one rank of a passive each (Improved Death Strike,
  Sudden Doom, Chilblains, Rage of Rivendare, Revitalize, Empowered Healing, Judgements of the Just, Lava Flows,
  Improved Healing Wave, Fire and Brimstone, Rend and Tear, Improved Mangle). Below 60 the key talents come earlier
  than the profiles had them (Mind Flay at point 11 instead of 24, Last Stand at 11 instead of 28, ...): 44 of the 46
  templates changed their order (the rogue's two Combat templates did not). Not taken on purpose: talents no rotation
  of mod-playerbots casts (Guardian Spirit, Lightwell, Aura Mastery, Focus Magic ...), Silence (its prerequisite, two
  points of Improved Mind Blast, is not in the shadow profiles), Dancing Rune Weapon (the Blood templates keep five
  points in Frost, so the tree has 46). The module SQL `ga_spec_respec_20261125.sql` (characters, applied once by the
  DB updater) marks every bot on a changed template for a free reset at its next quiet moment; a dungeon role's
  second spec is reset by itself at the next entrance (it no longer fits its template).
- **Class defaults** (`is_default`): warrior Arms, paladin Retribution, hunter Beast Mastery, rogue Combat, priest
  Shadow, death knight Blood, shaman Enhancement, mage Frost, warlock Demonology, druid Feral.
- **Of a guild** (characters table `ga_guild_spec_template`, ids from 100000, at most 40 per guild): the talents a
  character has now, saved as a template (`SpecService::SaveFrom`). The order is derived: the tree with the most points
  first, row by row, a talent after the one it depends on. So the leader builds a spec in the normal talent window of
  a character he plays and saves it.

## Which template a bot plays by

`ga_bot_spec.template_id`: 0 or no row = the default of its class, 1 = manual (the module spends no points for this
bot), else a template id. `SpecService::GetEffective`.

- **Maintain** (`OrderService::UpdateBot`, on the first tick after a login, after a level-up, and every five minutes;
  out of combat; not for the guild leader): the bot spends its free talent points along its template, skipping points
  it has already and points it cannot learn. The first time the module sees a bot (no row yet), talents that do not fit
  its template are reset once, for free: that is how the bots mod-playerbots had specced move to their class default.
  Later a bot's talents are never reset by this step, so points the leader spent by hand stay.
- **Assign** (`SpecService::Assign`, the leader's choice): the bot's talents are reset for free and learned again by the
  new template at once; a bot in a fight or dead gets it at its next quiet moment (the planner looks every second
  while `SpecService::HasPendingReset`; log line "gets the talent template ... at its next quiet moment", then the
  reset line), a bot that is not in the world at its next login (`reset_pending`). "Manual" resets nothing.
- After a reset, the first points, or a change of the tree with the most points, `BotDriver::ResetStrategies` lets
  mod-playerbots choose its strategies again; the planner puts its own strategy profile back on its next tick.
- `resetTalents` dismisses a hunter's or warlock's pet (the core does); the bot calls it again.

`Playerbots.AutoPickTalentsForPlayerControlledBots` must be 0 (`tools\configure-server.ps1` writes it; the module warns
at startup otherwise): with 1 mod-playerbots spends the points first.

## Commands

| Command | What |
|---|---|
| `.ga spec [bot]` (GM, console) | the bot's talents and template, and the templates it can play by |
| `.ga admin spec <bot> <id \| default \| manual>` | assign (administrator, console, SOAP) |
| `.ga admin specsave <character> <purpose 0-3> <name>` | save the character's talents as a template of its guild |
| `.ga admin specdelete <leader> <id>` | delete a template of the guild; its bots go back to the class default |
| `.ga bot plan <bot>` | ends with the talents and the template |

Activity log: `<bot> (level 12) talents reset, 3 talent points spent by "Leveling: Shadow": 0/0/3`.

## Limits and open points

- The rotations stay mod-playerbots' own (its strategies per tree); the wowbot335 fight classes are written for another
  engine and are not ported.
- Below level 10 a bot has no talents and mod-playerbots plays its default tree for the class.
- Only the active talent group is handled (no dual spec), no glyphs, no pet talents.
- A raid purpose of its own has no built-in templates yet (the `group` profiles serve dungeons and raids).
- The hunter's good pet at level 60 and the beast master's exotic one use the template (docs/orders.md "Class
  abilities").
- Protocol and addon: see docs/protocol.md "Talent templates" once the Talents sub-tab is built.
