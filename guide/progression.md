# Progression

Your guild grows in two ways: **renown** unlocks bot slots, a longer offline window and features; **guild points** buy
perks that help every member. Both come from what the guild does.

## Renown

Your bots earn renown for the guild:

| What | Renown |
|---|---|
| A bot gains a level | 10 |
| A bot completes a quest | 3 |
| The guild earns a new achievement (see below) | its achievement points, at least 5 |
| The guild reaches a tier of a tiered achievement | the tier's points |

Your own character earns no renown from levels and quests; its achievements count for the guild like everybody's.

Renown fills renown levels. The total renown a level needs:

| Level | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 | 8 | 9 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Total renown | 500 | 1,750 | 4,250 | 8,750 | 15,750 | 25,750 | 39,750 | 58,750 |

The server may scale all renown with a multiplier; the Overview and Progression tabs show your guild's real numbers.

## What renown unlocks

| Renown level | Bot slots | Offline window | Features |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 3 | 1 hour | Orders, recruitment, talent templates, gear management |
| 2 | 5 | | Profession plans |
| 3 | 8 | 2 hours | Autopilot limits |
| 4 | 10 *(100 gold)* | | |
| 5 | 15 *(200 gold)* | 4 hours | |
| 6 | 20 | | |
| 7 | 25 *(500 gold)* | 6 hours | |
| 8 | 30 | | |
| 9 | 39 *(1,000 gold)* | 8 hours | |

With 39 bots and you, a guild has 40 members.

### Buying unlocks

The slot steps marked with gold are bought: once your guild has the renown level, press **Buy** on the **Progression**
tab. The gold comes from your guild bank (the bot setting **Unlocks.PayFrom** can make it the guild bank first and then
your purse, or your purse alone). **Buy** stays greyed while the payer has too little. A purchase cannot be undone.

A step the guild has more slots than already (because renown gave 20 slots before you bought the 15) shows as
**Included** and is not sold.

## Guild achievements

Achievements belong to the whole guild. When you or a bot earns one, every member gets it in its own achievement list,
and the guild gains renown and guild points the first time. Members that are offline get it when they next log in.

## Tiered achievements

The **Achievements** tab lists series of goals in tiers. Each tier gives guild points and the same amount of renown; the
last tier you can reach at level 60 gives double.

| Series | Tiers | Points per tier |
|---|---|---|
| Highest level of each class and of each race in the guild | 10, 20, 30, 40, 50, 60 | 5 |
| Highest skill of each profession | 75, 150, 225, 300 | 5 |
| Items in the guild storage, gold of the guild | from 100 up to 1,000,000 | 10 |
| Creatures killed, per creature type | from 100 up to 1,000,000 | 10 |
| Quests completed by the guild | from 10 up to 100,000 | 10 |
| Dungeon runs (the last boss down) | 5, 25, 50, 100, 500 | 10 |
| Raid runs | 1, 5, 10, 25, 50 | 20 |

Tiers beyond level 60 are shown greyed; they wait for content the game does not cover yet.

## Guild perks

Guild points are the points of your guild's achievements and tiers. Spend them on the **Perks** tab; a perk works for
you and every bot at once and for good. Perks with ranks cost 50, 100, 200, 350 and 500 points per rank.

| Perk | Effect per rank (5 ranks) |
|---|---|
| Swift Feet | +5 % movement speed |
| Fast Learners | +10 % experience |
| Deep Pockets | +5 % looted gold |
| Thorough Hands | 5 % chance of one more crafting material |
| Second Wind | after a kill, 1 % health and mana over 10 seconds |
| Lucky Finds | +5 % drop chance for items up to rare |
| Master Crafters | +10 % crafting speed |
| Quick Hands | +10 % mining, herb gathering and skinning speed |

| Perk | Cost | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Material Haulers | 100 | Loot goes into the guild storage by itself (see [Storage and gold](storage-and-gold.md)) |
| Gills | 100 | Breathe under water |
| Feather Light | 100 | No fall damage |
| Reagent-Free | 150 | Class spells need no reagents |
| Cartographers | 250 | Every flight path of your faction is known |
| Instant Flights | 400 | Flights from a flight master land at once |

Some servers give every flight path and instant flights to everybody anyway; the last two perks change nothing there.

**Material Haulers is the first perk to buy**: without it, what your bots loot stays in their bags.
