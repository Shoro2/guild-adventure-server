# Taking control

You can play any of your bots yourself, with its own bags, spells, talents, action bars, quest log and pet. Neither
character logs out: the one you leave goes on with its order while you play the other.

## Taking over a bot

1. Press **Play** on the roster, or **Take control** in the bot's details.
2. Your client goes to the character screen. The bot is listed there although it lives on another account.
3. Select it and press **Enter World**. You are now playing the bot where it stands.

While you play a bot, the [control bar](the-window.md#the-control-bar) at the top of the screen offers **Return to
leader**, a quick switch to another bot, **Watch / Take over** and **Details**. Going back to your leader or on to
another bot works the same way, through the character screen.

Good to know:

- **In a fight** a switch is refused. The addon keeps your request and switches as soon as the fight is over ("Waiting
  for the fight to end" on the control bar); press the button again to cancel it.
- A switch is also refused while either character is flying, on a vehicle, in a duel or trade, charmed or dead.
- Your account needs a free character slot (at most 10 characters per account) for the bot to be listed.
- If you log out while playing a bot, the bot goes back to its order and plays on. Your leader logs out with you
  unless it has an order of its own ([Orders](orders.md#your-leader)).

## Watch mode

While you play a bot you can let the AI play it on with its order and just watch: press **Watch** on the control bar
(or `/ga watch`, or its key binding). The character stays yours on the screen; its movement keys are locked while the
AI plays. **Take over** gives you the controls back at once. Switching to another character ends watch mode.

Your leader can be watched too once it has an order other than **Hold position**.

## Playing along in a dungeon run

Your own character can join a dungeon run of your bots, played by you or by the AI, and you can switch between the two
at any time: see [Dungeons and raids](dungeons-and-raids.md#playing-along).
