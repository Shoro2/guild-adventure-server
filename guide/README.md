# Player guide

Guild Adventure is a guild management game on a World of Warcraft 3.3.5a server. You are the leader of an adventurers'
guild. Up to 39 guild members are bots; with you that is a guild of 40. You decide where they level, which professions
they learn, which talents they play, which groups they form and which dungeons and raids they go into. At any time you
can take over one of them and play it yourself. The guild starts small, grows with what it achieves, and keeps playing
for a while after you log out.

This guide explains the game from a player's point of view. How the server works inside, how to set one up and how the
code is built is the technical documentation in [`docs/`](../docs/README.md).

## The game in one minute

1. Create a character. It becomes your guild leader and founds the guild the first time it enters the world.
2. Open the window with `/ga` or the minimap button and recruit three bots on the **Recruitment** tab.
3. They start on the **Autopilot**: they level on their own, quest, visit vendors and trainers, and put what they loot
   into the guild storage.
4. Watch them on the **Roster**, give them other orders, plan their professions and talents, and take one over with
   **Play** whenever you like.
5. What the guild does earns **renown**. Renown unlocks more bot slots and a longer offline window; gold buys more.
   Achievements earn guild points for **perks** that help every member.
6. From the low-level dungeons to the raids of level 60, the **Dungeons** tab sends groups of your bots into
   instances, with or without you.

The game covers classic content up to level 60. It is an alpha: expect rough edges and bots that sometimes do something
odd.
